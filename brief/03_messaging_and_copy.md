> Cedars Trail Riding is Lebanon's first eco-equestrian tourism experience: guided horseback
> adventures across the Shouf mountains — from a gentle one-hour ride to an epic 8-day trek — that
> reconnect people with nature, support local communities, and build confidence through a real
> bond with horses.

through the cedars.

## B. Brand pillars (from their own copy)

1. **First & authentic** — "Lebanon's first equestrian tourism experience", "first of its kind in
   the country".
2. **Eco & responsible** — "eco-equestrian tourism", "minimize environmental impact", "support
   local communities".
3. **Reconnection** — "reconnect with nature", "peaceful escape", "space to grow, reflect, improve".
4. **For everyone** — "whether it's your first time or you're a seasoned rider", families, kids,
   couples, groups, corporates.
5. **Safety & care** — "hand-picked for safety", "trained guides, proper gear, helmets, safety
   briefings", "well-trained horses".

---

## C. Suggested tone of voice
- Warm, nature-forward, adventurous but calm.
- Short sentence punch + sensory detail (cedars, canter, mist, valley).
- Bilingual vibe (EN primary; AR support) — hospitality-first.
- Avoid: hyper-American "ranch" style; this is Lebanese mountain eco-tourism.

## E. Words to keep straight (naming)
- Brand: **Cedars Trail Riding** / **CTR** — the equestrian product.
- Parent: **Cedars Xtreme** — umbrella adventure brand ("Managed by Cedars Xtreme").
- Legal-ish: "Cedars Trail Riding Club" (older name in directories).
- Region spelling varies across sources: **Shouf / Chouf** both used — pick one for consistency
  (Shouf is common in English tourism copy) and note the other for SEO.
- Beiteddine is spelled "Beit Eddine" on their site — keep their spelling for that trail name.
