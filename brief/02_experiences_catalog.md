## A. Product architecture

**Two product families:**

1. **Trail Rides / Treks (core)** — hour-long to multi-day horseback experiences.
2. **Experiences & Events (secondary)** — non-standard bookings layered on the same horses/trails.

## E. Experience-level ladder (design suggestion, derived from data)

1. **First ride / beginners** → Pine Trail, Beit Eddine Trail (calm, guided, basics taught)
2. **Scenic leisure** → Panoramic Trail, 1-hour trail rides
3. **Adventure** → Rocky Trail, Custom Trails, Barouk cedars canter
4. **Immersion** → Marj Bisri (1–3 days, riverside camping)
5. **Epic expedition** → multi-day up to 8 days across the mountains

## F. Unknowns blocking precise product pages
- Exact price per ride/tier (USD and/or LBP and/or fresh USD).
- Per-ride duration in hours/minutes and distance in km where applicable (only Marj Bisri has a
  stated duration).
- Group size min/max; age min; weight limit (TripAdvisor says one person per horse, ages 6–99).
- What's included at each tier (meals? camping gear? photography?).
- Multi-day format: where do riders sleep (camps, guesthouses), meals, luggage, guide ratio.
- Add-on list possibilities: coffee/tea stop, photo package, transport from Beirut, combined
  Beit-eddine/Moussa Castle visits.
- Seasonality per trail (snow closure, summer heat) and booking lead times.
