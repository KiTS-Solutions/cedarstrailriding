## 7 · Blog / Portfolio Write-Up

*[PHOTO: homepage hero, full width]*

**Cedars Trail Riding Website: Building a Site That Matches the Experience**

**The Client and the Challenge.** Cedars Trail Riding offers guided horseback riding experiences
across Lebanon, operating out of Samqaniyeh in the Shouf District of Beiteddine. It's the kind of
business that lives or dies on trust: customers choose to show up somewhere unfamiliar and put
themselves in your hands — and before they do, they look you up online. What they find matters.

The challenge wasn't that Cedars Trail Riding lacked something real to offer. They have beautiful
horses, genuine landscape, and the kind of experience people remember. The challenge was giving all
of that a digital home that felt worthy of it — and clearly communicating the full scope of a
business operating across an entire country.

**The Approach.** From the start, the goal was clarity and warmth in equal measure. Visitors needed
to arrive, feel the place immediately, understand what was on offer, trust the people behind it,
and know exactly how to take the next step. We built a full multi-page website structured to move
visitors naturally from discovery to contact. Every section earns its place.

*[PHOTO: 'Meet Our Horses' or contact/booking area]*

The contact approach was a priority. Multiple phone numbers, an email address, and a social media
handle are all clearly presented — different customers reach out in different ways, and removing
that friction is one of the simplest things a website can do for a service business.

**The Design Details.** The palette is deep olive green and warm cream — not arbitrary. Olive green
carries associations of landscape, nature, and steadiness; cream keeps it from feeling heavy or
corporate. Together they create something rooted and welcoming, exactly the tone a business like
this needs.

*[PHOTO: section showing olive green + cream palette — footer or full-bleed band]*

Typography is clean and readable across all screen sizes — important for customers searching on
mobile while planning a trip. Headings bold and confident; body copy effortless to read. Real
photography carries the emotional weight: genuine images of horses and Lebanese terrain communicate
authenticity that no copywriting can replicate.

One detail worth calling out: the Lebanon map in the footer. A simple, clean outline of the country
with a location pin communicates immediately that Cedars Trail Riding is not a single-location,
single-trail business. They operate nationally. The map makes that legible in seconds.

**The Result.** *[PHOTO: polished full-page scroll / footer with contact + brand info]*

The finished website gives Cedars Trail Riding a digital presence that matches what they actually
offer. Visitors arrive, feel the landscape, understand the business, and know how to reach out. The
site works as a genuine business tool, handling the trust-building work that happens before any
conversation starts.

**Closing.** This is the kind of project that shows what a well-built website can do for an
experience business. If you run a tour company, an outdoor activity business, or any service where
customers need to trust you before they show up, your website is one of your most important assets.
If you're ready to build something that works as hard as your business does, get in touch.
