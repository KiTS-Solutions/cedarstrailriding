- [ ] **Groups & events** — school group, corporate, celebration (3)
- [ ] **Horses** — individual portraits (count TBD) + names/personalities

- [ ] **Brand extras** — favicon, color guide, any existing brand PDF
- [ ] **Testimonial verbatim text** — approve/replace paraphrases in `08`/`09`

## Formatting requests
- Photoshop/RAW masters if possible; deliver web sizes too (1600px+ at 72dpi).
- Prefer landscape for heroes/banners; portrait for horse cards.
- Filename convention: `trail-pine.jpg`, `horse-name.jpg`, `gallery-01.jpg`.

## Source tracking (legal)
- Record photo credits if any. Confirm the client owns/operates Instagram + TripAdvisor imagery
  before hotlinking or re-hosting.
